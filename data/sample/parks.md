# Maplewood Parks and Recreation

Maplewood maintains three public parks and a network of walking trails along the
river. The parks are free to enter and open from dawn until dusk every day.

## Riverside Park

Riverside Park is the largest green space in town, with a playground, a duck
pond, and a covered picnic area that can be booked for events. Dogs are welcome
but must be kept on a leash near the playground.

## Hilltop Park

Hilltop Park offers panoramic views of the valley and a popular sledding hill in
winter. It has an outdoor fitness area with free-to-use exercise equipment and a
small community garden tended by volunteers.

## Sports facilities

The town sports field next to Hilltop Park has two football pitches and a tennis
court. The tennis court can be reserved online for one-hour slots. A community
swimming pool is open from June to September, with reduced-price entry for
children and seniors.
