# Maplewood Recycling and Waste Collection

Maplewood runs a weekly waste collection service for all households. Knowing
which bin goes out on which day keeps the streets tidy and the collection
trucks on schedule.

## Collection schedule

General waste is collected every Tuesday morning. Recycling (paper, glass, and
plastic) is collected every Thursday morning. Garden waste is collected on the
second and fourth Friday of each month, from March to November only.

Bins should be placed at the curb before 7:00 on collection day and brought back
in by the evening.

## What can be recycled

Clean paper and cardboard, glass bottles and jars, and rigid plastic containers
can all go in the recycling bin. Please rinse food containers before recycling
them. Plastic bags, polystyrene, and broken glass cannot be recycled at the curb
and should be taken to the recycling center on Mill Road.

## Recycling center

The recycling center on Mill Road is open every day from 8:00 to 18:00. It
accepts electronics, batteries, paint, and bulky items such as old furniture at
no charge for Maplewood residents.
