# Maplewood Public Library

The Maplewood Public Library sits on Cedar Street, next to the town hall. It is
the oldest public building in Maplewood, opened in 1932 and renovated in 2018.

## Opening hours

The library is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 to 19:00, and on Saturday
from 10:00 to 16:00. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Membership

Residents of Maplewood can register for a free library card at the front desk.
A library card lets you borrow up to ten items at a time, including books,
audiobooks, and board games. Loans last for three weeks and can be renewed twice
online unless another member has reserved the item.

## Services

The library offers free public computers, wireless internet, a quiet study room,
and a children's reading corner. Every Wednesday afternoon there is a story-time
session for young children, and on the first Saturday of each month a local book
club meets in the study room.
