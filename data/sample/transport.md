# Getting Around Maplewood

Maplewood is a compact town, and most destinations are within a short walk or
bicycle ride. For longer journeys, the town runs a small bus network and a
shared bicycle scheme.

## Local buses

Two bus lines serve Maplewood. Line A runs from the train station to the
hospital every twenty minutes on weekdays, and Line B connects the town center
to the residential neighborhoods every thirty minutes. Buses run from 6:00 to
22:00. A single ticket costs two credits and can be bought from the driver, and
a day pass costs five credits.

## Shared bicycles

The town operates twelve bicycle stations where residents can pick up and drop
off shared bicycles. The first thirty minutes of each ride are free; after that
a small per-minute fee applies. A monthly subscription removes the per-minute
fee entirely.

## Parking

Street parking in the town center is limited to two hours between 8:00 and
18:00. The covered car park on Station Road offers all-day parking and is free
on Sundays.
